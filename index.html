Whoami

Bahraini penetration tester with a First-Class Honours degree in Networks & Cybersecurity (90% overall) from Northumbria University, and hands-on testing experience in Bahrain and the UK.

Most of my time goes on the offensive side: web and infrastructure assessments, API testing, and the OWASP Top 10 against real clients. I've done it at Vulnxperts for enterprise clients and at the NEBRC for small businesses across North East England. I'm just as careful with the report as with the exploit, because a finding only matters once someone can act on it.

Off the keyboard I'm an ex-national basketball player (GCC U16 gold with Team Bahrain) and a former student council president. These days I'm grinding Hack The Box toward the OSCP.
